Works the way each document is used

DocumentWhat it prints
InvoiceBill-to and ship-to, dates, source and reference, sections and notes, discounts and taxes per line, partial payment and amount due, amount in words, bank details with payment reference, scan-to-pay QR, payment terms, authorised signature.
Sales OrderOrder and delivery dates, salesperson, customer reference, section subtotals, the customer's online signature with name and time.
Request for QuotationNo prices. Expected date and quantity per line, blank price and lead-time columns for the vendor to fill in, quote deadline.
Purchase OrderVendor product codes, buyer, vendor order reference, expected arrival, Incoterm, delivery instructions, authorised buyer signature. The taxes column hides when no line is taxed.
Delivery NoteOrdered against delivered quantities, lot numbers, carrier, tracking and weight, backorder table for what is still to come, the receiver's signature.
Picking ListScannable document barcode and product barcodes, from and to locations, lots and packages, tick boxes, a stock-shortage note, picked-by and checked-by lines.

Options in every design

Content

  • Product images on lines, on or off
  • Amount in words, in the customer's language
  • Company representative signature printed on every document, or a blank line for a wet signature
  • Watermark such as ORIGINAL or COPY
  • Status stamp from the record, such as Delivered

Look

  • Font family from Odoo's report fonts: Lato, Roboto, Open Sans, Montserrat, Oswald, Tajawal
  • Font size: small, normal or large
  • Three footers: standard, detailed (address, contacts, bank, tax ID, tagline) or minimal
  • Right-to-left layout for Arabic, Hebrew and Persian, mirrored automatically

Two pages and more

  • Header and footer repeat on every page, with page x of y
  • The table header repeats at the top of each new page
  • A row is never split, and a section title never ends a page
  • Totals, payment details, terms and signature move to the next page together
  • Title, addresses and stamp print on the first page only

Built for Odoo's PDF engine

  • A4 portrait, using Odoo 19's fixed 52 mm header and 32 mm footer bands
  • Layout made of tables only: no flexbox, grid or CSS variables, which wkhtmltopdf cannot render
  • Rotations carry the -webkit- prefix; images are embedded, not linked
  • Colours and font come from the company settings, like Odoo's own layouts
